WITNESS STATEMENTS — CASE 003: THE JACKPOT HEIST

STATEMENT 1 — Overnight Pit Boss, Carlos Mendez
"Tommy came in through the main entrance around 12:30 AM. I was surprised to see him —
he doesn't usually come in that late, especially on a Saturday. He nodded at me but
didn't stop to chat. He went straight to the elevator. He looked... tense. Like he had
something on his mind. I didn't see anyone follow him."

STATEMENT 2 — Parking Attendant, Jerome Williams
"I was on the overnight shift at the executive parking garage. I saw Ms. Zhao's black
Lexus pull in around 1:10, maybe 1:15 AM. That was unusual — she doesn't usually work
weekends. She came back to her car around 2:30 AM. She was walking fast. She had a
leather messenger bag that I don't think she came in with."

STATEMENT 3 — IT Database Administrator, Sarah Kim
"The deactivated keycard issue? Yeah, that's weird. When an employee is terminated,
their keycard should be deleted from the system within 48 hours. Miguel Santos's card
was flagged for deletion but put on 'department hold.' That's a feature only department
heads and above can use. We don't log who places the hold — it's a known gap in the
audit trail. I flagged it to my manager six months ago but nothing was done."

STATEMENT 4 — Gaming Commission Investigator, Harold Burke
"Tommy reached out to me on March 15th. He said he'd found what he called 'ghost accounts'
in the VIP credit system — player profiles with credit lines but no real players behind them.
He was meticulous. He had printouts, spreadsheets, everything. He said the full documentation
was in the casino vault and his home office. We were supposed to meet Monday morning.
Obviously, that meeting never happened."

STATEMENT 5 — Estate Attorney, Patricia Hwang
"Tommy came to my office three weeks before he passed. He wanted to change his will.
He was emotional — said his nephew had 'a problem' and needed 'tough love.' He reduced
Luca's inheritance from 60% to 5%. He specifically said not to tell Luca until after
the amendment was finalized. As far as I know, Luca was never formally notified."
