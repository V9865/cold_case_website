is significantly behind.

scholarship fund at UNLV. Luca reportedly did not know about the amendment.

Tommy's phone records show a 14-minute call with Luca on the evening before the

Get help or you're on your own."

ALIBI:

The casino is 15 minutes from Caesar's Palace by car.

Luca's vehicle was not recorded in the Caesar's Palace parking garage that night.

Luca has no criminal record. No known violent tendencies.
