BELLAGIO BACKSTAGE AREA — TECHNICAL REPORT
Prepared for: Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department

The Bellagio fountain show backstage area consists of three main zones:
1. The rehearsal stage and dressing rooms (Ground Level)
2. The fountain control room (Sub-Level 1)
3. The HVAC and mechanical plant room (Sub-Level 2)

The fountain control room is a sealed, climate-controlled space measuring 12x8 meters.
It houses the fountain sequencing computers, audio mixing boards, and lighting control panels.
The room has one main entrance (keycard-locked steel door) and one maintenance hatch
leading to the mechanical plant room below.

The ventilation system for the control room is managed by an independent HVAC zone (Zone C).
The external HVAC control panel is located in the backstage corridor, 15 meters from the
control room door. It can be manually overridden with a physical switch.

The main door has an emergency lock feature that can be activated from inside the room
or remotely via the technical director's admin console.

Security cameras cover the backstage corridor, the rehearsal stage entrance, and the
parking garage access. The control room interior has no camera coverage.
Camera feeds are stored on a local NVR server accessible from the IT office on Level 2.
